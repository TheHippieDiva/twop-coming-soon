EP 4 · OCT 21 · 45 min · with Amanda

What Actually Saved Us in Prison (It's Not What You Think) https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9IwPUCbgEE

Three women on what actually got us through. Mail call and the people on the outside. Three-minute phone calls rationed across the month. Books, journaling by hand, music on an MP3 player that cost more than it should.

And laughing. We never laughed so hard in our lives, and we were sober. For families: blunt advice on what to put on the books, and why staying connected is the whole game.
