For families

Spending limit. The BOP sets a monthly commissary spending limit, and some items have their own limits (shown on the list). Check the facility's current list and handbook.
Money on their books. Use only the BOP's official ways to send money (listed on the BOP's communications and money page). Never send cash in the mail.
Mail basics. Use the person's full name and register number and the facility's mailing address from its official BOP page. Follow the facility's mail rules, or the mail comes back.
